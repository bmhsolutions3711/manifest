# Manifest

**Know what you're carrying.**

## The name

In freight, a **manifest** is the list of what is actually on the load — not the fantasy, not the rumor, the cargo that ships.

In life and company-building, a **manifest** is also a declaration: what you stand for, what you will learn, what you refuse to waste miles on.

This product is both:

1. **Your intelligence cargo list** — briefings, books, playbooks worth the weight  
2. **Your founder declaration** — high-signal only; empty miles for the mind are deadhead  

## Tagline

> Know what you're carrying.

Supporting lines (use sparingly):

- Open when you want. No pings.
- High-value miles for your attention.
- Built for builders on the move.

## Why not “AI Pulse” / “Radar”

- **Pulse** is a feature, not a brand — every health app owns it  
- **Radar** is already a local project name in this stack  
- **Manifest** scales: trucker today, category brand tomorrow (knowledge ops for operators)
